# The Last Bargain — Game Overview

*The Last Bargain* is a single-player, top-down boss-rush game set in a colosseum where every victory comes with a price. The player must survive four boss fights, but ordinary upgrades are not enough: to earn the coins needed for preparation, the player can sell parts of their own build, including stats, equipment, and even powerful resources. Once sold, these things do not return during the run. Between fights, the player can bargain at the Demon King’s or Archangel’s beacon, while the Storekeeper demands rent and keeps a record of the player’s choices.

The game uses three themes from the brief. **Contracts** are represented by bargains, permanent sales, and rent owed to the Storekeeper; each deal has an immediate benefit and a lasting cost. **Chain Reactions** appear when one sale or purchase changes the player’s options in later rounds, and the game’s receipts explain the consequences of those choices. **Contagion** appears through demon bargains that spread rot across owned items and through wolf attacks that can infect parts of the player’s interface. These systems make preparation part of the challenge rather than a separate menu: the player must decide what they can afford to lose before entering the next fight.

The goal is to balance combat skill with risky resource decisions. A run can become easier in one way and more dangerous in another, so success depends on understanding how today’s bargain affects tomorrow’s fight.
