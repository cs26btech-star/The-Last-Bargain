MUSIC SHOPPING LIST (all from OpenGameArt.org, all CC0 = public domain, no credit required but nice)
=======================================================================================================

HOW TO USE
1. Open the link, press the download button for the file named below.
2. .ogg, .mp3 and .wav all work as they are and loop automatically. A .wav is big (the boss battle one is ~20 MB), so converting
   it to .ogg is nicer (Audacity: File > Export > Ogg Vorbis). Do NOT just rename a .wav to .ogg: that breaks it.
3. Rename it to the EXACT name in the "save as" column and drop it into this audio/ folder.
4. Open Godot once so it imports the files. Missing files are fine: that scene is just silent. Press F9 in the game to check.
5. Paste a credit line for each track you really use into MUSIC_CREDITS in scripts/game_data.gd.

I picked tracks uploaded in 2021 or earlier on purpose, so there is no doubt they are not AI-generated
(your rules ban AI-generated assets). Check the licence box and the AI tag on each page anyway before you submit.
