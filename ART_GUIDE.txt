THE LAST BARGAIN - ART GUIDE

The full list of every picture slot (and where it appears in the game) is in ASSET_LIST.txt.

Short version:
- Open the .png files in Krita. Files ending in .png.import are Godot's cache; ignore them.
- Save your drawing over the file with the EXACT same name, transparent background, any size.
  The game scales it into a fixed box. Keep the feet/base in the same place across poses.
- Missing file = coloured box or placeholder. Nothing crashes.
- Shopkeeper pictures are placeholders on purpose: save figure_shop_demon.png, figure_shop_angel.png,
  figure_catalogue.png and figure_lore_archangel.png and they appear automatically.
